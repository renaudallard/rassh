rassh is an SSH client built around the real OpenSSH 10.6p1 and LibreSSL 4.3.3. There is no SSH reimplementation: ssh, sftp, scp, ssh-agent, ssh-add and ssh-keygen are the OpenSSH programs, so what works with ssh on a computer works here, ~/.ssh/config included.

FILE TRANSFERS WITH YOUR PHONE'S STORAGE
• sftp and scp work straight from your phone's folders: Download, DCIM, Documents or any other
• Copy whole folders with put -r, get -r and scp -r, or many files at once with wildcards
• A file browser over SFTP to browse, download, upload, rename, delete and create folders
• This needs All files access, asked when you open sftp or scp without it. Without it, transfers work from the app's private folder

TERMINAL
• xterm compatible terminal with colours and alternate screen, for vim, tmux, htop and the like
• Two rows of keys a phone keyboard lacks: Esc, Tab, Ctrl, Alt, arrows, Home, End, Page Up and Down
• Several sessions at once, kept open in the background
• A host option to attach to your last tmux session or start one

HOSTS
• Saved as Host blocks in ~/.ssh/config: user, port, identity file, local, remote and dynamic forwards, jump hosts, and any other ssh_config option
• Reordered by holding and dragging a host, the order kept in ~/.ssh/config
• Your own blocks, Host * and Match included, are left as they are, apart from IdentityFile and CertificateFile lines kept pointing at your keys, and ssh checks the file before it is saved
• Quick connect with plain ssh arguments, like -p 2222 me@example.org or -J jump host

KEYS
• Generate ed25519, ecdsa, rsa and post-quantum mldsa44-ed25519 keys, or import your own
• Private keys with their public key move into a vault encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore and unlocked with your fingerprint, a few, such as links, staying in clear
• Each connection gets its own ssh-agent, emptied once ssh is logged in, or after 60 seconds for sftp, scp and shared connections, so jump hosts, sftp and scp use the keys without writing them to disk
• A warning when a server's host key changes, with the new fingerprint to confirm

LOCAL SHELL
• Android's own shell and toybox, with the OpenSSH programs first in PATH, and access to your files with All files access

MOVING TO ANOTHER PHONE
• Hosts, keys and font size exported in one file encrypted with a passphrase

PRIVACY
No ads, no analytics, no tracking. rassh connects only to the servers you choose.
